1. **Audience and the one feeling.**
Mac owners on Apple silicon who already pay for an AI subscription (ChatGPT Plus or Pro) or run local models, and who are uneasy about handing their files to a vendor's cloud computer: privacy-minded prosumers, freelancers, self-hosters, open-source believers. Secondary: people bounced off Hermes Agent or OpenBot because setup felt like devops. The one feeling at the end: *this one is mine.* Calm ownership. They should believe a team of helpers runs on their Mac, on the model they already pay for, stops when they say stop, and the source is public to check. Ease is the proof style: two clicks to first chat, no sneering at anyone.

2. **Three differentiators.**
- **Your model, including the one you already pay for.** Sign-in with a subscription, an API key, or a local runtime. Comparison table, "Models" and "Reuses what you pay for": Grok Bot is xAI only, Muse is Meta Muse Spark only, Dots is GPT-6 Astra only. OpenBot and Hermes also take any model, so the edge over them is the route, not the idea: section 1 claim 7, two-step first run in a native SwiftUI app ("Native Mac app" row: they are Electron or CLI wrappers).
- **Your Mac, your data, no account of ours, and the source is public.** "Where work runs", "Where data lives", "Source" rows: Grok Bot, Muse and Dots execute on vendor cloud computers and store there; we execute and store locally, AGPL-3.0, no account system. OpenBot is source-available under PolyForm Noncommercial, not open source. Always paired with the caveat: cloud models and connected apps receive what you send them.
- **A team you control, with three clear access levels.** "Team of bots" and "Control" rows: Dots launches one dot, Muse one agent; OpenBot runs full access with no per-action approval. We ship bots with own instructions, memory, routines and model, groups of 2 to 6, plus Read only / Auto / Full access and one-action approval cards (section 1 claim 4, claim 5).

3. **The demo.**
One workflow, zero to working teammate, all shipped today, all demo data. (a) First run: Welcome, Get started, Connect, Sign in on the ChatGPT row with the official 1.1.2 flow. (b) Dark home: click the real starter chip "Create a copywriter bot for my brand". (c) Generalist answers and docks a proposal card; click Add teammate; a Copywriter bot with a pastel avatar appears in the rail. (d) In its chat: attach a synthetic brand-kit folder from the "+" menu, open the Model chip to show its own model, set Auto. (e) Ask it to draft a launch post and sketch a header idea; the Excalidraw MCP call in Auto raises an approval card above the composer; click Approve; an interactive drawing card and a draft page card land in chat. (f) Library, Show in Finder: files sit in ~/Documents/Useful Bot. (g) Bot settings: instructions, Memory showing one note, avatar colour change. (h) Open the Permission menu, pick Read only, ask for an edit, the bot declines in plain text. This single pass proves model choice and ease (a, d), team plus control (b, c, e, h) and local storage with open source closing it out (f, end card).

4. **Arc and motion design.**
Quiet open, brisk middle, settled close. 0 to 8 s slow: black, logo tile, room tone. 8 to 68 s: cuts land on clicks and card appearances, each shot 4 to 8 s, cursor leads every cut. 68 s onward: stillness. Transitions are hard cuts and matched-content cuts (same element, new zoom); one crossfade only, light to dark theme. No whip pans, no zoom bursts, no speed ramps. 2D throughout; the only depth is the app's own soft long shadows; skip the three package entirely. Recreated moments (model row with LM Studio Qwen3.8-27B, the closing three-bot rail) use real tokens: radii 24 and 16, pill composer, 248 pt rail, SF Pro inside UI frames, Inter Semibold with -0.018em tracking for the wordmark only. Captions: Inter, sentence case, max two lines, ink on a translucent surface token, bottom safe area. Mascot use: the animated SVG as the open and close tile on black only; inside the app, the avatars' real float-and-blink while working, recorded, never redrawn; one blink cutaway synced to the "you approve it" line. Sound, no music: low room tone bed; UI clicks taken from the app where capturable, else foley; soft air whooshes on scene changes; a distinct double tick on approval cards; two seconds of true silence before the closing line. Nothing melodic, nothing rhythmic.

7. **Avoid.**
Name no competitor on screen or in VO; comparisons on their turf (computer use, always-on cloud) are fights we cannot win honestly, and "unlike X" sneers against the ease note. Never say first, only, always on, works while your Mac sleeps, nothing leaves your Mac unqualified, available worldwide, no telemetry as an absolute, ten bots at once as a benchmark, notarized, one-click Gmail, Claude subscription, or imply Intel, Windows or iPhone. Show no computer use, no browser agent, no Copilot row, no Settings > Computer tab, no dev-build strings ("Useful Bot Dev", "WJ"), no untracked owner screenshots. Clichés to cut: neon grids, particle logos, hologram typing, terminal rain, "meet the future", seamless, supercharge, unlock, speed-ramped cursors, stock hands on keyboards. Visual traps that make us look like the others: cloud-globe imagery, pulsing always-on dots, cyberpunk colour grade over a near-monochrome UI, and zooms so tight the rail disappears.
